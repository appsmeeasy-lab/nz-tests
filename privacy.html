Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains what the NZ Citizenship Test iPhone app ("the app") does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect personal information and has no accounts.

What the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device only:

  • your answers, statistics, study streak and game scores;
  • questions you saved or missed;
  • your settings, such as chosen topics and a planned test date.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app or use Reset progress in the app settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of your information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can send you alerts, such as when the official test questions are published. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

The app is a general study tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we will update this page and change the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: appsmeeasy@gmail.com.
